GNSS IMU Logger – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

GNSS IMU Logger ("the app") is published by tethered.systems ("we", "us"). It records satellite-navigation, motion-sensor and cellular-network data on your phone for navigation research and teaching, as a companion to the book Introduction to Inertial Navigation and Kalman Filters. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes, and the choices you have.

In short: the recordings the app makes stay on your phone. We do not run any server, we do not receive your recordings, and there are no accounts. The app contacts third parties only to download satellite data, for correction services you configure yourself, and to show advertising (Google AdMob).

1. Data the app records on your device

When you press Start Logging, the app writes a log file to the app's own folder on your phone (Android/media/com.gnssimulogger/GnssImuLogs). A log file can contain:

DataWhy
Precise location: raw GNSS (GPS, Galileo, BeiDou, QZSS, GLONASS) measurements and the phone's own position fixes The purpose of the app: recording and computing your position and trajectory
Motion sensors: accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, barometer Inertial navigation and sensor-fusion research
Cellular network information: identities of nearby mobile-network cells, signal strengths and timing advance Research into positioning from mobile-network signals
Correction data received from a correction service you configure (NTRIP), and downloaded satellite orbit data Improving position accuracy
Camera feature positions (only if you switch on camera tracking): the pixel coordinates of tracked image features and the camera's calibration. Camera images are processed on the phone and are never stored, logged or transmitted. Research into camera-aided (visual-inertial) navigation
Demo video (only if you switch on "Record a demo video"): an MP4 recording of the camera view, saved beside the log on your phone. It can show people, vehicles and number plates nearby. It is never uploaded by the app. Making demonstration videos of the navigation results
Phone model and Android version; the session's settings Interpreting the recording correctly
Diagnostic and crash reports (text files) Troubleshooting

Because a log contains your precise location over time, it can reveal places you have been. Log files are not uploaded anywhere by the app. They leave your phone only if you choose to copy or share them, for example to analyse them on a computer. Treat them as you would any location history.

The app also stores its settings on your device, including any correction-service credentials, access token and OpenCellID key you enter a learned magnetometer calibration and a cache of looked-up cell positions.

2. Network connections made by the app

ServiceWhenWhat is sent
Satellite orbit data downloads: BKG (igs.bkg.bund.de) and NASA CDDIS (cddis.nasa.gov) While logging, about every 10 minutes Standard web requests for public files. CDDIS requests include the NASA Earthdata access token you entered. No location data is sent.
NTRIP correction service (a caster you choose and configure) While logging, if you enabled it Your caster user name, password and chosen stream (mountpoint). The app does not send your position to the caster.
SUPL assistance server (default supl.google.com) Only if you enable SUPL in the settings (off by default) An approximate location (latitude/longitude), which the protocol requires to return the satellite data for your area.
OpenCellID (opencellid.org, operated by Unwired Labs) Only if you enter an OpenCellID API key (off by default) Your API key and the identity of the mobile-network cell your phone is connected to (country, network, area and cell number), to look up the cell's estimated position. A cell identity indicates your approximate location. Results are cached on your phone so each cell is looked up only once.
Google AdMob (advertising) Whenever ads are shown See section 3.

Every connection reveals your IP address to the server contacted, as with any internet use. These services are operated by third parties under their own privacy policies.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived), device and app information, and interactions with ads. The SDK is not given access to the app's log files or GNSS data. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your phone).

4. Permissions

  • Location (precise): to read GNSS measurements, position fixes and cellular-cell information.
  • High sampling rate sensors: to record motion sensors at full rate.
  • Camera (optional): only if you switch on camera tracking, to track features in the view ahead of a phone mounted on the windscreen. Images never leave the processing step on the phone.
  • Internet / network state: for the downloads, correction services and ads described above.
  • Foreground service (location, and camera when camera tracking is on), notifications, wake lock: to keep logging while the screen is off, with a visible notification while it runs.

Location is used only while you are logging, and a notification is shown for the whole time.

5. Sharing and sale of data

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share your recordings with anyone. The only data shared with third parties is that described in sections 2 and 3.

6. Retention and deletion

Log files and settings remain on your phone until you delete them. You can delete individual log files with any file manager, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on request. Data collected by Google for advertising is retained under Google's policies.

7. Children

The app is a technical tool for adults and students of navigation engineering and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Security

All network connections use encryption where the service supports it (HTTPS for downloads; TLS for SUPL). NTRIP casters commonly use unencrypted connections, a limitation of the NTRIP protocol, so use a password that is unique to your caster account.

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above.
